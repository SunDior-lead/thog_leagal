Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data The House of Gratitude ("we", "us", "our") collects through the The House of Gratitude mobile app and website (the "Service"), why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the Service you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information we collect

We only collect what we need to run the Service:

  • Account information: your full name, email address and password. Your password is stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash; we never store or see it in plain text.
  • Verification codes: temporary 6-digit codes emailed to you to verify your email address or reset your password. They expire shortly after they are issued.
  • Mobile number and country: only if you choose to register for a course, so that the course team can contact you about it.
  • Journal entries: the date, reflections, gratitude notes and mood you write in your private journal.
  • Stories: the title, text and category of gratitude stories you choose to share with the community. Stories are shown anonymously by default.
  • Reports: if you report a story, we record the story, the reason, any description you add, and your account so our moderators can follow up.
  • Activity within the Service: courses you register for and articles you have marked as read, so we can show your progress.
  • Technical data: when your device talks to our servers, our hosting provider automatically processes your IP address, request time and similar technical logs for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

We do NOT collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone input, or advertising ID. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep you signed in, the app stores your session tokens and a copy of your basic profile in the app's private storage on your device. It also caches community links to load faster. This data is removed when you log out or uninstall the app.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, verify your email and let you reset your password.
  • To provide the features you use: your journal, community stories, affirmations, articles and course registrations.
  • To contact you about a course you registered for, or to reply when you contact us.
  • To moderate community content, investigate reports and keep the community safe.
  • To protect the Service against fraud, abuse and security incidents, and to comply with law.

We do not sell your personal data, rent it, or use it for advertising. We do not use your journal entries for any purpose other than showing them to you.

4. Who can see your information

  • Journal entries are private to your account. They are not shown to other users.
  • Stories you share are visible to other users of the Service. When a story is anonymous, your name and email are not shown with it.
  • A small number of authorised administrators can access account and content data where needed to operate the Service, moderate content, handle course registrations or respond to support and legal requests.

5. Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the Service, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — hosting, database and server infrastructure. Data is stored in the AWS Asia Pacific (Mumbai) region.
  • Our email delivery provider — to send verification and password reset emails.
  • Content delivery networks — some images in the app are loaded from third-party image servers, which receive your IP address as part of the request.

When you open an external link from the app (for example WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube or Zoom), that service's own privacy policy applies.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order or a valid request from a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or others.

6. Security

All data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are hashed, data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider, and access to production systems is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a personal data breach where the law requires.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Account data, journal entries and stories are kept while your account is active.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 14 days of you confirming the request, except where we must keep limited records to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent abuse.
  • Verification codes expire automatically. Server logs are kept only for as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

8. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and get a summary of how it is processed.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Delete individual journal entries or stories from within the app at any time.
  • Delete your account and associated personal data.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (this will not affect processing already carried out, and some features may stop working).
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To delete your account, open Profile → Delete Account → Delete Now in the app, or email thehouseofgratitude1111@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account with the subject "Account Deletion Request - The House of Gratitude" and tell us why you want to leave. Our team will get in touch within 7 days, and your account and personal data will be deleted within 14 days of you confirming the request.

To exercise any of your other rights, email thehouseofgratitude1111@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We will verify your request and respond within 30 days.

9. Children

The Service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. International users

We are based in India and our servers are located in India. If you use the Service from another country, your data will be transferred to and processed in India.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app or by email. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact and grievance officer

For questions, privacy requests or complaints, contact our Grievance Officer at thehouseofgratitude1111@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days.
