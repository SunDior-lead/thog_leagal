Account and Data Deletion

If you use The House of Gratitude, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Send an email from your registered email address to:

thehouseofgratitude1111@gmail.com

Use the subject: Account Deletion Request - The House of Gratitude.

What happens after your request?

Our team will contact you within 7 days. After you confirm your request, your account and associated personal data will be deleted within 14 days, except for information we are legally required to retain or need to retain for permitted security purposes.

You can also delete your account directly within the app through Profile → Delete Account → Delete Now.

For questions, contact: thehouseofgratitude1111@gmail.com
